How to read this

The accessible-toilet gap counts No and Partial among all assessed facilities. Other availability gaps follow the workbook definitions and may include missing responses. Monthly teleconsultations describe service volume.

The thin black mark on each bar is the Maharashtra-wide level, so you can see at a glance whether a district is better or worse than the state.

Subcentre figures exist for 35 of the 54 gap indicators. Staffing, stock-outs and most equipment questions are asked at PHC level only.

PHC list shows each PHC with the subcentres linked to it. Some subcentres could not be matched to a parent PHC yet and are listed separately for review. Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban had no assessments. Very small districts can swing to 0% or 100% on a few units, so confirm extreme values with the district office.
