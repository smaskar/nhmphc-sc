Source, coverage and matching rules

Each facility contributes its latest eligible submitted or verified FY 2026–27 assessment in the backup. Reopened and test records are excluded. The denominator for each priority gap is all assessed facilities in the selection. Missing or unrecognised answers appear separately. A facility can have several gaps.

The mapping queue starts with 1,992 registered subcentres that lacked a confirmed parent after the first reconciliation. A backup match requires every recorded parent label to identify the same unique PHC in that district and block after standardising service qualifiers such as PHC, tribal, IPHS and 24×7. Spelling suggestions require district confirmation. The counts describe this dated backup and reconciliation. The facility registry is updated separately.
