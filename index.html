How to read this

Every number is a gap: the share of assessed PHCs that have the problem, so a lower number is always better. Where the question was "is it available?", PHCs that answered No or gave no information count as gaps.

The thin black mark on each bar is the Maharashtra-wide level, so you can see at a glance whether a district is better or worse than the state.

Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban had no assessments. Very small districts can swing to 0% or 100% on a few PHCs, so confirm extreme values with the district office. Source: PHC analysis, all districts workbook.
